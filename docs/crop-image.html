FAQ

How do I crop a photo?

Choose your image, pick a shape, drag the photo to frame it and press Download cropped image.

Does cropping reduce quality?

No. The crop is cut from your original pixels, so the quality stays the same.

Can I crop a profile picture?

Yes. Choose Square 1:1, which is what most apps use for profile pictures.

Is my photo uploaded?

No. Everything runs on your device.

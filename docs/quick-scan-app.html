Quick Scan - Free Document Scanner for Android

Scan documents, ID cards and notes with your phone camera and save them as PDF. Free, no watermark, no signup. Your scans stay on your phone.

Download Quick Scan (APK, 40 MB)

How to install

  1. Tap the download button above and wait for the download to finish.
  2. Open the downloaded file (Files app, APKs or Downloads).
  3. If your phone asks, allow installing from this source, then tap Install.
  4. Open Quick Scan from your app list.

Features

  • Auto edge detection and crop
  • Multi-page scans to one PDF
  • Import from gallery
  • Share your PDF anywhere

Privacy

Your documents are never uploaded. The app shows Google AdMob ads, which may use your device advertising ID. See our Privacy Policy.

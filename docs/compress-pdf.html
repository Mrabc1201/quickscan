FAQ

How do I reduce a PDF to 200 KB or 500 KB?

Choose your PDF, pick the target size and press Compress. The tool lowers image quality step by step until the file is under your target.

Will the text still be selectable?

No. To reach small sizes the pages are saved as images, so the text cannot be selected or searched afterwards. Keep your original file if you need to edit it.

Is my PDF uploaded?

No. Compression happens in your browser on your own device.

Why can't it reach my target size?

Very long PDFs or very small targets may not be reachable. Try a larger target or split the PDF into parts.

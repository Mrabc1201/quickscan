How do I add page numbers to a PDF?

Choose your PDF, pick the position and style and press the button. Download the numbered PDF.

Can I start from a number other than 1?

Yes. Change the Start at box, for example to 5.

Does it work with scanned PDFs?

Yes. The numbers are added on top of each page.

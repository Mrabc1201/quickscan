FAQ

How do I make a photo black and white?

Choose the photo, pick Grayscale and download it.

What is the Document style for?

It raises the contrast so a photo of a page looks cleaner and uses less ink when printed.

What does the threshold do?

In pure black and white mode, brighter pixels become white and darker ones become black. Move the slider until it looks right.

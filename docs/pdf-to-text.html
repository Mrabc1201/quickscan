FAQ

How do I copy text from a PDF?

Choose the PDF and press Extract text. The text appears in a box that you can copy, or download as a TXT file.

Why is no text found?

Scanned PDFs and photos of pages contain pictures, not text, so there is nothing to extract. You need OCR software for those.

Is my file uploaded?

No. Everything runs in your browser.

Does it keep formatting?

No. You get plain text only, without fonts, tables or images.

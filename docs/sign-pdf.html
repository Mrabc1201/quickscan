FAQ

Is it really free with no watermark?

Yes. You can sign and download your PDF with no watermark and no signup.

Is my document uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything happens inside your browser on your own device. Your PDF never leaves your phone or computer.

Can I sign a PDF on my phone?

Yes. Open this page on your phone, choose the PDF, draw your signature with your finger, drag it into place and download the signed file.

Is an electronic signature legal?

In the US (ESIGN Act) and UK (Electronic Communications Act 2000) a simple electronic signature is generally valid for many documents, but some documents need witnesses, notarisation or a certified signature. Check with the person or office asking you to sign.

FAQ

How do I rotate a photo?

Choose the photo and tap Rotate left or Rotate right until it looks right. Then press Download.

What is the difference between rotate and flip?

Rotate turns the picture. Flip makes a mirror image, left to right or top to bottom.

Does it lower the quality?

Quality stays almost the same. JPG photos are saved at high quality.

Is it free and private?

Yes. Your photo is never uploaded.

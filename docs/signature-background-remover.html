FAQ

How do I make my signature transparent?

Sign on white paper, take a photo, choose it here, move the slider until the paper disappears and download the PNG.

Yes. Choose black or blue, or keep the original colour.

Where can I use the PNG?

You can insert it into Word, Google Docs, Canva or any editor. It has no background, so it sits cleanly on the page.

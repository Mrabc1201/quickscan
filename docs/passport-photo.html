FAQ

What size is a US passport photo?

2x2 inches (51x51 mm). This tool exports 600x600 pixels, which is the size the US State Department accepts for online uploads, and keeps the file under 240 KB.

What size is a UK passport photo?

35x45 mm. This tool exports 413x531 pixels at 300 dpi. The same size is used for Schengen visas and many other countries.

How do I take a good photo at home?

Stand in front of a plain white or light wall in even daylight, face the camera straight, keep a neutral expression, no glasses, and have someone take it from about 1 metre away.

Does this remove the background?

No. It does not change your background, so take the photo against a plain white or light wall. Always check the official requirements of your country before submitting.
